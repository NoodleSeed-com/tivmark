# Equipment requests in Tivmark

## Categories

Every equipment request falls into one of six categories:

| Category | Typical items |
| :-- | :-- |
| `LAPTOP` | Work laptops and docking stations |
| `MONITOR` | External displays |
| `PHONE` | Work phones and tablets |
| `PERIPHERAL` | Keyboards, mice, headsets, webcams |
| `FURNITURE` | Desks, chairs, standing desk converters |
| `OTHER` | Anything that does not fit the categories above |

A request carries a category, a free-text item description, and a quantity. The item is
free text on purpose — teams stock different things, and Tivmark does not impose a
catalogue.

## The lifecycle

A request moves through three stages:

1. **`PENDING`** — submitted, waiting for a reviewer.
2. **`APPROVED`** or **`DECLINED`** — a team owner or admin has decided.
3. **Fulfilled** — the approved item has actually been handed over. Fulfilment is a
   separate step from approval, because approving a monitor and having a monitor on the
   desk are different facts and teams need to see the gap between them.

A requester can cancel their own request while it is pending.

## Who can do what

- **Members** raise requests and see their own.
- **Owners** and **admins** additionally see the team queue, approve or decline, and mark
  items fulfilled.

Roles are per team. Someone can be an admin of one team and a member of another, and
Tivmark applies the right permissions in each.

## Requesting equipment

A request needs a category, an item, and a quantity. If any of those are missing, Tivmark
collects them in a short form rather than guessing.

Like booking leave, submitting an equipment request is a confirmed action — the exact
category, item, and quantity are shown for confirmation before anything is created.

## Common questions

**Can I request several different items at once?** Each request covers one item and a
quantity. Raise separate requests for different items so they can be approved and
fulfilled independently.

**Can I ask for something not in the categories?** Use `OTHER` and describe it in the item
text.

**What if my request is declined?** The decision is recorded against the request. Raise a
new request if circumstances change; Tivmark keeps the original rather than removing it.

**Does Tivmark track serial numbers or asset tags?** Not today. Tivmark tracks the request
and its fulfilment, not the asset lifecycle after handover.
