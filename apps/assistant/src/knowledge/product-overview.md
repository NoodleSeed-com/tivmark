# What Tivmark does

Tivmark is a people-ops portal for growing teams. It replaces the spreadsheets, chat
threads, and email chains that companies usually use to track who is off and who needs a
laptop, with self-serve requests and one-click manager approvals.

Two workflows are live today:

- **Time off** — people check their balance, book leave, and cancel a request they no
  longer need. Managers review a queue for their team.
- **Equipment** — people request hardware, track where a request has got to, and cancel it
  if plans change. Managers review and then mark items fulfilled.

More people-ops workflows are planned. Everything in Tivmark is scoped to a team, so a
company with several teams keeps separate policies, separate queues, and separate
approvers.

## Who it is for

Companies large enough that "just ask your manager in chat" has stopped working, but not
so large that they want a full HRIS rollout. Typically 20–500 people.

## What makes it different

- **Per-team policies and roles.** Allowances, approvers, and equipment categories are set
  per team rather than company-wide, so an engineering team and a support team can run
  different rules in the same workspace.
- **One-click approvals.** A reviewer sees the request, the requester, the dates or the
  item, and the remaining balance in one place, and approves or declines without leaving
  the queue.
- **Enterprise-ready foundations.** SAML single sign-on, SCIM user provisioning, API
  keys, webhooks, and a REST API are part of the product rather than an upgrade tier.
- **Agent-accessible.** Tivmark's capabilities are available to AI assistants through the
  Model Context Protocol, so people can ask for a balance or book leave in conversation
  instead of navigating the UI.

## What Tivmark is not

Tivmark is not payroll, not an applicant tracking system, and not a performance review
tool. It does not calculate pay, taxes, or statutory entitlements — it tracks the requests
and approvals your team makes against the allowances you configure.
