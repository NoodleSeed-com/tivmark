About

About Tivmark

Tivmark is a people-ops portal that keeps the everyday requests — time off, equipment, and the approvals behind them — in one simple place.

Most growing teams manage these in a tangle of spreadsheets, chat messages, and email threads. Tivmark replaces that with self-serve requests, clear per-team policies, and one-click approvals, so nothing gets lost and everyone knows exactly where a request stands.

Time off and equipment requests are live today, with more people-ops workflows on the way. It all runs on an enterprise-ready foundation — SSO, SCIM, audit logs, and a full REST API — so Tivmark fits neatly alongside the tools your team already uses.
