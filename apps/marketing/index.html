About Tivmark

Tivmark is an AI-powered Action Desk that keeps customer, public, and employee requests — plus the work behind them — in one simple place.

Most growing teams manage these in a tangle of spreadsheets, chat messages, and email threads. Tivmark replaces that with self-serve requests, clear per-team policies, and one-click approvals, so nothing gets lost and everyone knows exactly where a request stands.

Sales, support, software access, general requests, time off, and equipment are live today. It all runs on an enterprise-ready foundation — SSO, SCIM, audit logs, and a full REST API — so Tivmark fits neatly alongside the tools your team already uses.
